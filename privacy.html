BuiltByFailures Publisher (Posthouse)

Privacy Policy

BuiltByFailures Publisher, also called Posthouse, is a desktop publishing app that runs on the operator’s computer. Posthouse uses YouTube API Services to connect YouTube channels, publish videos, and show channel and video activity.

Data Posthouse accesses and uses

When you connect YouTube, Google provides authorization tokens and Posthouse reads your channel ID, channel name, and available profile details. It uses this information to identify the selected channel. Posthouse reads video details and available statistics to show posts and performance. When you use playlist or comment features, it reads the playlists, comments, and replies needed for those features.

When you choose to publish to YouTube, Posthouse sends the selected video, title, description, thumbnail if supplied, and publishing settings to YouTube. It can also send playlist updates, replies, or deletion requests that you authorize. Google handles sign-in and receives these requests through its services.

Storage and sharing

Windows encrypts the saved Google authorization tokens on the computer that runs Posthouse. Posthouse stores connection information, posting history, and analytics snapshots locally. Local backups may include the database and encrypted credential files. The app refreshes the displayed analytics when it can fetch current data.

Posthouse uses Google account data to provide the YouTube features described here. It sends requests and content selected for YouTube to Google. Posthouse does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles your information and the YouTube Terms of Service for terms that apply to your use of YouTube.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

You can revoke Posthouse’s YouTube access in Google Account permissions. To request deletion of saved YouTube data, including relevant data in local backups, or to ask a question about this policy, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Google consent screen for BuiltByFailures Publisher.
